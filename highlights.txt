Highlights

- A 2007 family of copulas for aggregation processes is revisited and corrected
- Kendall's tau and Blomqvist's beta of global copulas never increase with dimension
- Their limits are explicit probabilities given by the de Finetti representation
- One family of copulas attains every Kendall's tau sequence of global copulas
- Spearman's rho of Gumbel and Clayton copulas has positive limits in high dimension
